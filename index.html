Questions

Simple answers.

I'm not technical. Can I still work with you?

Yes, that's most clients. Tell us about your business in your own words and we'll handle the rest, explaining everything in plain language.

How much will it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we don't list fixed prices. Message us, share your requirements, and you'll get a clear price before any work begins.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, logo if you have one, photos or products you want shown, and an idea of what you'd like to achieve. We'll guide you on the rest.

How long does it take?

Reels and posts usually take a few days. Websites and apps take longer depending on size. You'll get a timeline up front.

Can I ask for changes?

Of course. We share drafts early and refine them with you.

Who will I be talking to?

Jarvis.SI or a member of the team, directly, usually on WhatsApp or whatever is easiest for you.
