Questions

Simple answers.

I'm not technical. Can I still work with you?

Yes, that's most of our clients. You tell us about your business in your own words, and we handle everything else and explain it plainly.

How much will it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we don't list fixed prices. Message us, tell us your requirements, and you'll get a clear price in writing before any work begins.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, logo if you have one, photos or products you want shown, and an idea of what you want to achieve. We'll guide you on the rest.

How long does it take?

Reels and posts are usually a few days. Websites and apps take longer depending on size. We'll give you a timeline up front.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. We share drafts early and make changes with you until it feels right.

Who will I be talking to?

Ayushman or a member of his team, directly. Usually on WhatsApp, whichever is easiest for you.
